The harness calls `.backward()` on the tensor returned by `train_step`, but the current implementation already calls `loss.backward()` internally and then returns `loss.detach()` — a tensor with no grad_fn. The fix is to remove the backward/optimizer logic from `train_step` and return the raw loss.
